The tweak that seemed quick. The prompt that asked for more context. The brief that changed after it was approved. Anyone who works with development, AI and marketing recognizes these scenes before they finish reading.

That routine is where **Off Script** came from — our proposal for a collection and brand kit. The idea is to take Atto's personality to T-shirts, hoodies, objects and stationery, with pieces people actually want to wear every day.

[Explore the full gallery and download the files](/en/brand-kit/).

## A quiet front. A back with something to say.

The rule for the apparel is simple: a small official logo on the front, room for expression on the back. The fabric stays part of the design. The brand shows up without taking over the whole piece.

Graphite, white and green tie the collection together. The typography organizes the main phrase and leaves a second reading for whoever comes closer. The prints are in Portuguese: on <span lang="pt-BR">“Só mais um ajuste”</span> (“Just one more tweak”), for example, the small print says “Small scope. Long story.” On <span lang="pt-BR">“Na minha máquina funciona”</span> (“Works on my machine”), the note is “The sample is small.”

The joke comes from the process of creating. It's a conversation between people who have been there.

## AI with context and 37 attempts

The AI T-shirts have two different compositions. On the black one, <strong lang="pt-BR">“Não é magia. É prompt.”</strong> (“It's not magic. It's a prompt.”) crosses out the word magic and pairs the phrase with 37 attempt boxes. The last one turns green. The footer admits: “The first version doesn't count.”

On the white one, <strong lang="pt-BR">“Meu prompt tem mais contexto que eu”</strong> (“My prompt has more context than I do”) appears inside a file window. Even so, the small comment warns: “It still asks for more details.”

## Marketing with provisional approval

<strong lang="pt-BR">“O briefing era outro”</strong> (“The brief was different”) got a tilted APPROVED stamp, with an asterisk. The file is called `final_final_agora_vai` (roughly “final_final_this_is_it”). The condition for approval sits right below: “Until the next meeting.”

The other T-shirt has <strong lang="pt-BR">“Meu signo é teste A/B”</strong> (“My sign is A/B testing”), with two contrasting blocks and “Rising sign: conversion” as a second line. The composition uses the comparison between versions itself to build the print.

## Two hoodies, two drawings

On the black hoodie, <strong lang="pt-BR">“Fonte: vozes da minha rede neural”</strong> (“Source: voices from my neural network”) comes with a diagram of connections. The indicators complete the meme: “Confidence: 100%. Sources: 0.”

On the white one, <strong lang="pt-BR">“Orgânico só o café”</strong> (“Organic? Only the coffee”) comes with an engraved coffee maker. The steam from the cup turns into a rising chart, and the text wraps it up: “Reach is something we boost.” The illustration gives the piece a different language from the black hoodie's diagram.

## The identity also lives in the objects

The set includes cards, a notebook, a welcome box, stickers, a tote, a cap, a bottle, a mug and digital applications. The intention is for the materials to talk to each other: the green from the print shows up again on the card's edge and inside the box; the discreet brand runs through paper and fabric.

The edition brings together **30 pieces and applications, 43 artworks and eight mockups**. The gallery lets you filter by AI, marketing and type of piece, enlarge the artwork and download the SVGs. The full package includes versions with editable text, fonts, a PDF catalog, an inventory and a production guide.

The mockups were created with the help of AI to present the visual direction. The collection still depends on samples, materials and technical sign-off for production; the images do not show products in stock. The white hoodie includes a raster illustration embedded in the SVG, with the prepress needs described in the guide.
